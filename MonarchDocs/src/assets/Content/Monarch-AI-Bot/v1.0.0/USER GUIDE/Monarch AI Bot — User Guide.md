# 1. Authentication and Account Management

Monarch AI Bot uses a temporary guest authentication system designed to provide users with access to the platform without requiring personal information such as email addresses or phone numbers.
Every account is associated with a unique username and an automatically generated UUID (Universally Unique Identifier).

## 1.1 Create an Account (Sign Up)

When you first access Monarch AI Bot, you will be presented with the **Create an Account** screen.

### Interface Elements

| Element        | Description                                             |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Username       | Enter a unique username of your choice.                 |
| Create Account | Creates your guest account using the provided username. |
| Log in         | Redirects existing users to the login screen.           |

### How to Create Your Account

1. Enter your preferred username in the **Username** field.
2. Click the **Create Account** button.
3. The system generates a unique UUID automatically for your account.
4. Your guest account is created with a temporary validity of 24 hours.
5. Your generated UUID will be provided to you.

### Important: Save Your Login Credentials

Once your account is successfully created, you will receive your unique UUID.

**Keep both your username and UUID safe.**

These are your login credentials, and you will need both whenever you want to access your account again.

- Username: The unique username you selected during registration.
- UUID: The unique identifier automatically generated by Monarch AI Bot.

There is no email-based or phone-based account recovery process described for this guest authentication system. Make sure you retain your credentials.

## 1.2 Login to Your Account

If you have already created an account, you can access it through the **Welcome Back** login screen.
### Interface Elements

| Element    | Description                                                       |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Username   | Enter the username you selected during registration.              |
| User UUID  | Enter the UUID generated when you created your account.           |
| Login      | Authenticates your credentials and grants access to your account. |
| Create one | Redirects new users to the account creation screen.               |
### How to Log In

1. Enter your registered username.
2. Enter your previously generated UUID.
3. Click the **Login** button.
4. The system validates your credentials.
5. Upon successful authentication, you will be redirected to the main Monarch AI Bot interface.

**Note:** Both the username and UUID are required to authenticate your account.

![[Pasted image 20261111182458.png]]

## 1.3 Temporary Account Validity — 24 Hours

Monarch AI Bot uses a temporary guest account system.
Every newly created account has a validity period of **24 hours**.
### How the Account Lifecycle Works

1. **Account Creation:** You register using a unique username.
2. **UUID Generation:** The system automatically generates your unique UUID.
3. **Account Access:** You can log in using your username and UUID.
4. **24-Hour Validity:** Your account remains accessible during its validity period.
5. **Automatic Expiration:** After 24 hours, your account expires.
6. **Data Deletion:** All associated account data is deleted from the database.

### What Happens After 24 Hours?

Once your guest account expires:

- You will no longer be able to access your account.
- Your previous username and UUID will no longer provide access.
- All associated account data will be deleted from the database.
- Your previous conversations and stored information will no longer be accessible.
- Neither you nor Monarch AI Bot can retrieve the deleted account information.
To continue using Monarch AI Bot, you will need to create a new guest account.

## 1.4 Important Things to Remember

| Item              | Information                                                                |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Account Type      | Temporary Guest Account                                                    |
| Registration      | Unique username                                                            |
| UUID              | Automatically generated                                                    |
| Login Credentials | Username + UUID                                                            |
| Account Validity  | 24 hours                                                                   |
| Expiration        | Automatic                                                                  |
| Data Retention    | Account data is deleted after expiration                                   |
| Account Recovery  | No recovery mechanism is provided in the current guest authentication flow |
**Privacy at its core:** Monarch AI Bot's guest authentication system is designed to minimize the personal information required to access the platform. You can use the chatbot without registering an email address or phone number.

# 2. Dashboard and Main Workspace

After successfully logging in, you will be redirected to the Monarch AI Bot main workspace.
The main workspace is the central area where you can interact with AI models, create conversations, access previous chats, and view your account information.
The interface is divided into three primary sections:
1. **Left Sidebar:** Provides access to new conversations, recent chats, and basic account information.
2. **Central Workspace:** Contains the AI chatbot interface, model selection, and message input area.
3. **Right Sidebar:** Displays your account profile information, settings access, and logout option.

The workspace is designed to provide a simple and accessible environment for interacting with different AI models.

# 3. AI Model Selection

Monarch AI Bot provides access to multiple Large Language Models (LLMs) through its external LLM service provider.
You can choose an available model before starting or continuing a conversation.

![[Pasted image 20261111191740.png]]

## 3.1 Model Selection Dropdown

The **Choose your LLM model** dropdown is located above the message input area.
Clicking this dropdown displays the models currently available through the application.
The list may contain multiple models with different capabilities and purposes.
### How to Select an AI Model

1. Locate the model selection dropdown.
2. Click the dropdown to view the available models.
3. Browse the available options.
4. Select the model you want to use.
5. Enter your prompt in the message input area.
6. Send your message to begin interacting with the selected model.

The selected model will be displayed in the model selection field.

## 3.2 Model Availability

**Important: Model availability is dynamic.**

The models displayed in Monarch AI Bot are provided through an external LLM service provider.
Their availability is subject to the provider's:
- Terms and conditions.
- Model availability.
- Service updates.
- Model deprecations.
- Changes to supported models.

Therefore:
- No particular model is guaranteed to remain available permanently.
- The available model list may change over time.
- Existing models may be removed or replaced by the service provider.
- New models may become available as the provider expands its offerings.
Monarch AI Bot aims to make additional models available as supported options evolve.

**Note:** The model list shown in the application represents the models available at the time of access. Your available options may differ from those shown in documentation screenshots.

# 4. Chat Interface and AI Interaction

The chat interface is the primary feature of Monarch AI Bot. It allows you to communicate with AI models by submitting prompts and receiving generated responses.

![[Pasted image 20261111191907.png]]

## 4.1 Understanding the Chat Interface

Once you start a conversation, the central workspace changes from the welcome screen to the active chat interface.
The chat interface contains:

|Element|Description|
|---|---|
|Conversation Header|Displays the conversation's identifying information.|
|User Message|Displays the messages submitted by you.|
|AI Response|Displays the responses generated by Monarch AI.|
|Message Input|Allows you to enter new prompts.|
|Model Selector|Allows you to select an available AI model.|
|Send Button|Submits your message.|

## 4.2 Sending Your First Message

Follow these steps to start interacting with Monarch AI:
1. Select an available AI model from the model dropdown.
2. Click the message input area.
3. Enter your question, instruction, or prompt.
4. Click the Send button.
5. Wait for the selected AI model to process your request.
6. The generated response will appear in the conversation window.

You can continue submitting messages within the same conversation.

## 4.3 Viewing AI Responses

Once your prompt has been submitted, the conversation interface displays:
- Your submitted message.
- The AI-generated response.
- The conversation history.

The messages are presented in a conversational format, making it easier to follow the discussion.
AI responses may contain explanations, structured information, numbered lists, and other formatted content depending on the response generated by the selected model.

## 4.4 Continuing an Existing Conversation

Monarch AI Bot maintains conversation history within the same chat.
This allows the selected AI model to receive the previous conversation context when processing subsequent messages.
For example:
1. You ask a question about a particular topic.
2. Monarch AI generates a response.
3. You ask a follow-up question related to the previous response.
4. The AI can use the earlier conversation context to understand your follow-up.

**Important:** Conversation context is maintained within the same conversation. Separate conversations have separate chat histories.
Starting a new chat creates a separate conversation rather than continuing the context of an existing one.

## 4.5 AI Response Accuracy

Monarch AI displays the following reminder within the chat interface:
_"Monarch AI can make mistakes. Verify important information."_
AI-generated responses may occasionally contain inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading information.
Always verify important information before relying on it for critical decisions.

# 5. Conversation Management

Monarch AI Bot allows you to create and manage multiple conversations within your guest account.
You are not restricted to maintaining a single conversation.

## 5.1 Creating Multiple Conversations

You can create as many conversations as you need during your account's validity period.
To create a new conversation:
1. Locate the **+ New Chat** button in the left sidebar.
2. Click the button.
3. A new conversation will be created.    
4. Select an available AI model.
5. Enter your prompt and begin interacting.
Each conversation maintains its own conversation history.

## 5.2 Accessing Previous Conversations

All your created conversations are displayed in the Recent Chats section of the left sidebar.
You can select any available conversation to return to it.
This allows you to continue previous discussions without having to start from the beginning.

## 5.3 Switching Between Conversations

To switch between conversations:
1. Open the Recent Chats section.
2. Identify the conversation you want to access.
3. Click the corresponding conversation entry.
4. The selected conversation will be displayed in the central workspace.
You can switch between your conversations whenever required, as long as your account remains valid.

## 5.4 Conversation Date and Time

Each conversation is displayed with date and time information.
This helps you identify and distinguish between different conversations.
The displayed timestamp represents the conversation's last updated time.
As you continue interacting within a conversation, its last updated time changes accordingly.

## 5.5 Conversation Context and Separation

Every conversation maintains its own history.
For example:
- Messages sent in Conversation A belong to Conversation A.
- Messages sent in Conversation B belong to Conversation B.
- The context of Conversation A is not automatically carried into Conversation B.
This separation allows you to maintain independent discussions for different topics and tasks.

## 5.6 Conversation Availability

All conversations are associated with your guest account.
Since guest accounts have a validity period of 24 hours, your conversations are also subject to the same account lifecycle.
Once your account expires, the associated conversations and their stored data are deleted.

# 8. Guest Account Lifecycle and Data Retention

Monarch AI Bot uses a temporary guest account system with a fixed 24-hour validity period.
All account information and associated conversations are subject to this validity period.

## 8.1 Account Validity

Every newly created guest account is valid for 24 hours from its creation time.
You can use the application throughout this validity period.

## 8.2 Account Expiration

Once the 24-hour validity period ends:
- Your account expires automatically.
- You can no longer authenticate using the expired credentials.
- Your previous conversations become inaccessible.
- All associated account data is deleted from the database.

## 8.3 Data Deletion

Monarch AI Bot automatically deletes the account and its associated data after the validity period.
Once deletion takes place:
- You cannot access your previous account.
- You cannot retrieve your previous conversations.
- Your previous UUID cannot be used to restore the account.
- The deleted information cannot be recovered by you or through the application.

To continue using Monarch AI Bot, you must create a new guest account.

## 8.4 Important Account Guidelines

|Guideline|Information|
|---|---|
|Account Type|Temporary Guest Account|
|Account Validity|24 hours|
|Login Credentials|Username and UUID|
|Credential Recovery|Not available|
|Multiple Conversations|Supported|
|Conversation Context|Maintained within individual conversations|
|Account Expiration|Automatic|
|Data Deletion|After account expiration|
|New Account|Required after expiration|

**Final Reminder:** Always keep a copy of your username and UUID, and remember that your account and conversations are temporary. Save any important information you need before your 24-hour account validity period ends.
